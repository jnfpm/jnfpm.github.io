MACHINE LEARNING II · ACADEMIC PROJECT

Customer Segmentation

Unsupervised Learning & Market Basket Analysis

Overview

This project focused on using unsupervised learning techniques to identify meaningful customer segments based on demographic, behavioural and purchasing information.

The analysis was extended through Market Basket Analysis, allowing product associations to be explored within the identified customer segments.

Data & Preprocessing

The project involved customer-level information together with transaction data.

  • Customer demographic and behavioural features
  • Purchasing information
  • Transaction baskets
  • Engineered customer features

The preprocessing included missing-value treatment, feature engineering, transformations and scaling.

Clustering Approach

Several unsupervised learning algorithms were explored and compared.

K-Means Hierarchical Clustering DBSCAN MeanShift Self-Organizing Maps

Cluster solutions were evaluated using measures such as inertia and silhouette score. Dimensionality reduction techniques including UMAP and t-SNE were also explored for visualization.

Market Basket Analysis

After identifying customer segments, the project investigated purchasing behaviour within those groups.

Apriori and association rules were used to identify frequently occurring product combinations and potential relationships between products.

Key Takeaways

This project allowed me to develop a deeper understanding of unsupervised learning and how different clustering algorithms can reveal distinct patterns in customer behaviour.

It also showed how customer segmentation can be combined with transactional analysis to generate more actionable business insights.
